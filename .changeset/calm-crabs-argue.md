feat: add performance budgets to `doctor` command. New `checks.performance` config in `doctor.json` with budgets for `maxJSBundleSize`, `maxCSSBundleSize`, `maxPageHTMLSize`, `maxImagesKB`, `maxBuildTime`, and `maxFontCount`. New `--budget` flag runs `checkPerformance()` against `.boltdocs/performance-metrics.json` generated by the build. Violations emit `DoctorIssue` with configurable severity and integrate with existing `failOnError`/`maxWarnings` CI gates.
